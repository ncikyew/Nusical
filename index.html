  • TransposeRewrite notes and key signatures for B♭, E♭, F and other instruments.

  • ChordsName a chord or tap its notes to see what it is and which scales to ad lib over it.

  • ScalesAny scale written out on the staff, at concert pitch or for your instrument.

  • TermsLook up Italian, German, French and Latin markings, including long phrases word by word.

  • MetronomeSteady clicks with accents, subdivisions and tap tempo.

  • TunerTune with your microphone, shown in your instrument’s key.

  • FingeringQuick links to trusted fingering, alternate and trill charts for woodwind, brass and strings.

  • ScoreUpload sheet music and get it back transposed for another instrument.

Fingerings, terms and tunings are checked carefully, but your teacher and your ears always have the final say. Music notation is drawn with the open-source Verovio engraver.
