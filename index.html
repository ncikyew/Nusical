Thank you for playing. 🎶

Quick multiple-choice quizzes to revise music theory. Each quiz has 10 random questions, with an explanation after every answer and your score at the end. Choose a grade for a mixed set, a topic to focus on one area, or ear training to practise listening.

Level

Listen and answer: each question plays by itself, and you can replay it as often as you like. Turn your sound on.

Please note: these are general practice quizzes, loosely based on the ABRSM Music Theory grades. They aren’t past papers or a mock exam, and real exams also include written tasks, such as composing a melody or completing harmony, that a quiz can’t test. Use them for revision alongside your teacher and the official syllabus. Not affiliated with or endorsed by ABRSM.
