Privacy policy

Effective 20 November 2026

Nusical needs no account and doesn’t ask for your name or email. Your settings stay on your device, the tuner’s microphone sound never leaves your device, and Google’s advertising, analytics and YouTube services collect some data as described below.

Who we are

Nusical is a free music app for players, available on the web and on Android, run by an individual developer in Singapore. Contact: nusical.music@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

Nusical saves your choices in your browser’s local storage so the app remembers them next time. This includes your instruments, last tab, notes you typed, drone and tuner settings, and quiz best scores. This information is not sent to us, and you can delete it at any time by clearing your browser or app data.

Microphone

The Tuner asks for microphone permission only when you press Start. The sound is analysed on your device to show the pitch. It is never recorded, stored or sent anywhere, and the microphone turns off when you stop the tuner or leave the tab.

Advertising (Google AdSense)

Nusical shows ads from Google AdSense to keep the app free. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technology to show ads based on your visits to this and other sites. Google’s use of advertising cookies lets it and its partners serve ads based on your visits to Nusical and other sites on the internet.

  • You can turn off personalised ads in Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors at aboutads.info.
  • Visitors from the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown.
  • Learn more in how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Analytics (Google Analytics)

Nusical uses Google Analytics to count which tabs and features are used, such as page views, quiz starts and button taps. This helps decide what to improve. Google Analytics uses cookies and collects data such as your approximate location, device type and browser. It does not receive your notes, recordings or anything you type. You can block it with the Google Analytics opt-out add-on or your browser’s tracking settings.

Videos (YouTube)

The Videos tab lists videos from the Nusical YouTube channel and plays them with YouTube’s privacy-enhanced player (youtube-nocookie.com). Loading the list sends a request to YouTube but no information about you. When you press play, YouTube may store information on your device and collect data about your viewing under the Google Privacy Policy and YouTube Terms of Service. Nusical saves the video list on your device for a few hours so it loads faster.

Links to other sites

Nusical links to outside pages, such as fingering charts, teaching articles and the PayPal donation page. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for how they handle your data.

Children

Nusical is a general-audience app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices and rights

You can clear your saved settings at any time, block cookies in your browser, and manage ad personalisation as described above. Under Singapore’s Personal Data Protection Act and similar laws, you can ask what personal data we hold about you or ask us to correct or delete it. Email us and we will reply within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
